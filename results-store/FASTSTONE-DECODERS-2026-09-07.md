# FastStone Image Viewer — deep-fuzzing its bundled decoders (2026-09-07)

FastStone Image Viewer (closed freeware, FSViewer.exe x86) ships its format decoders as
open-source DLLs. Harness Forge generated harnesses for the ones it can lift, NemesisForge
fuzzed them deep. Bugs in these are reachable THROUGH FastStone (it loads this exact code).

Bundled open-source DLLs found in `C:\Program Files (x86)\FastStone Image Viewer`:
  libwebp.dll 1.3.2, lcms2-2.dll 2.18, brotli{common,dec,enc}.dll, jxl.dll (libjxl),
  jxl_cms.dll, jxl_threads.dll, hwy.dll (Highway), libsharpyuv.dll, pdfium.dll 146.
  Plus FastStone's own closed fsplugin01..09.dll.

## Generator improvement this drove
brotli was REFUSED (its BrotliDecoderDecompress puts encoded_size BEFORE encoded_buffer).
app-lift now recognises length-before-buffer, guarded to genuine byte pointers so a const void*
config arg is not mis-paired with a preceding size. Committed (525 tests).

## Not generator-liftable (honest)
- libjxl (JPEG XL) and pdfium are the highest-odds targets (newest/most complex), but their
  decode APIs are STREAMING/handle-based (JxlDecoder*/FPDF*) -- a create-then-process call
  SEQUENCE app-lift does not lift -- and both are heavy C++/CMake builds with SIMD + codec deps.
  Their own OSS-Fuzz harnesses exist; a hand-written streaming harness would be the path.
- fsplugin01..09.dll are FastStone's proprietary decoders: no source, and coverage-guided
  binary instrumentation has no working Windows-ARM64 backend, so they can only be black-boxed.
